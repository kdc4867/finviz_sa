#### 테마 2: 일본 엔화의 지속적인 강세
**헤드라인**: *"Explainer: What is the yen carry trade?"*  
**헤드라인**: *"yen defies gravity"*

**헤드라인**: *"China's EV makers shift gears to focus on humanoids as car market slows"*
